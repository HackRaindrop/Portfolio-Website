Who I am

I'm a developer and designer creating games and software that feel good to use. My work spans rich media web apps, Android and iOS apps, 3D environments, and Unity projects.

When I'm not coding, I'm often tinkering with Puck server mods, refining UX in small tools, or collaborating on short game jam builds with other creators.

  • RIT B.S. in Game Design and Development
  • Stack JavaScript · C# · Unity · Flutter · Swift · C++ · Substance Painter · Maya
